QUESTION 3: Which season appears to have an impact on a customer's spending per customer as well as their frequency of purchases?
    According to the table for question three, the winter season had the highest overall revenue earned, while the fall season came in second. Why may this be the case? There could be a variety of reasons for the increase in overall revenue during the winter, but two that stand out in the promoted discounts and offers, along with the fact that it's the holiday and gift-giving season, could be a legitimate justification for a customer's need to spend money.

QUESTION 4: Are repeat purchases more common among consumers who provide higher ratings?
    According to the chart, clients who make repeat purchases from a business with the highest average number of purchases—seven—may indicate that they are satisfied with the products or services, which could result in their rating. Overall, customers with evaluations of four to five stars are more likely to make repeat purchases.

QUESTION 5: Is there a connection between desired shipping method and location?
    Standard shipping is the most popular method for delivering goods to consumers, with the highest rates in California, Alabama, and Delaware, according to the connection-based depiction. Overall, this indicates that some consumers would rather have regular shipping than quick delivery; however, may this be explained by seasonal or urgent circumstances?

QUESTION 6: Which item do consumers usually spend more or less on each transaction?
    The table shows that consumers spend more money on jeans, with an average of $41. However, there is a contradicting point: although consumers spend more money on jeans based on transaction count, this raises the possibility that some consumers may find the item's price point too high. In contrast, a consumer can spend an average of $38 on an item, which increases the volume of transactions for that item.
